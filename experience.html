Clinical & Research Experience

Visiting Medical Student

Mallinckrodt Institute of Radiology
During my time in the Interventional Radiology department at Washington University in St. Louis, I worked under Dr. Sasha and leading specialists including Dr. Mani, Dr. Atta, Dr. Azhar, Dr. Kim, and Dr. Berry-Tony. I gained hands-on experience with CT-guided biopsies, CVC and PICC line placements, angioplasties, and IVC filter placements. I also closely observed uterine artery embolization, GI bleeding embolization, sclerotherapy, radiofrequency and microwave ablation, percutaneous drainage procedures, and Y-90 radioembolization.

Clinical Observer

Lenox Hill Hospital
Under the supervision of Chief of IR Dr. Mark A. Westcott, Dr. Kirk, and Dr. Art, I observed Y-90 radioembolization, prostate artery embolization, uterine artery embolization, fluoroscopic-guided biopsies, and thrombectomies. I also gained experience in patient consultations, pre- and post-procedure care, and imaging techniques including fluoroscopy and ultrasound guidance.

Research Assistant

Lahore University of Management Sciences
Dr. Mirza's lab focuses on immune impairments in type-2 diabetes and their impact on pneumococcal infections, with the goal of developing more targeted vaccines for this high-risk population. I gained hands-on experience in immunology and bacterial pathogenesis, learned advanced lab techniques, and contributed to research with public health implications.

Project Director of Tuberculosis

Volunteer Force Against Hepatitis Transmission
Collaborated with colleagues on several TB projects including awareness campaigns and innovative ideas to help the Government and people of Pakistan fight tuberculosis.

Local Exchange Officer (LEO)

IFMSA-Pakistan
Served as LEO for the Standing Committee on Professional Exchange. Responsibilities included communicating with representatives from other medical schools nationwide and facilitating clinical exchange programs. Participated in monthly Free Eye Camps and contributed to the medical awareness newsletter on public health topics.

General Secretary

KEMCISION Surgery Interest Group, KEMU
Fostered a collaborative environment promoting learning and skill development among aspiring surgeons. Contributed leadership and organizational skills to advance the interest group and create impactful experiences for members.

Student Volunteer

Alkhidmat Foundation Pakistan
Volunteered to raise funds for flood-affected communities during Pakistan's 2022 flooding. Organized a university booth and fundraising event whose proceeds were donated to Al-Khidmat Foundation.

Student Ambassador

Shaukat Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital
Represented the hospital at events to promote cancer awareness. Organized awareness campaigns and fundraising initiatives, provided guidance to cancer patients and families, and developed communication and leadership skills through diverse community engagement.
