Research foundation

Laboratory work

My early research training at King Edward Medical University and the LUMS Biology Lab built a foundation in translational science and laboratory methods, including tissue handling, cell culture, flow cytometry, PCR and RT-PCR, western blotting, microscopy, ELISA, histopathology, tissue engineering, genetics, and forensic laboratory workflows.

Perspective

Why IR

I was drawn to Interventional Radiology because it uniquely combines procedural skill with longitudinal patient care. I am especially interested in anatomy, pathology, physiology, pharmacology, and the way IR continues to evolve alongside AI and modern medicine.

Beyond clinical research, I follow AI, robotics, startups, and digital health—and enjoy turning ideas into useful websites and applications.
