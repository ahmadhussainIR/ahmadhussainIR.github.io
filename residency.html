How to read these numbers

Fill rate is not applicant match rate. It describes how many available positions were filled—not the chance that any individual applicant matched.

Categorical and advanced are different starts. A categorical program includes the clinical year; an advanced position begins at PGY-2 and requires a separate clinical-year plan.

Ranking data need context. NRMP applicant counts can include anyone who ranked at least one program in that specialty, even when it was not their first choice.

Applicant type is descriptive. These totals show who filled positions nationally. They do not capture program-specific screening, visa policy, clinical experience, or individual strengths.

Before applying

Check every program individually.

There is no single universal program deadline or eligibility policy.

  • 01

    Application deadline and whether the program reviews applications received after September 23

  • 02

    Integrated IR, DR, ESIR, independent IR, and linked preliminary or transitional-year structure

  • 03

    Program signaling participation and instructions for home or away rotators

  • 04

    USMLE or COMLEX expectations, graduation-year rules, clinical experience, and letter requirements

  • 05

    IMG eligibility, ECFMG status, accepted visa types, and sponsorship limitations

  • 06

    Interview dates, open houses, second-look policy, resident contacts, and coordinator email
