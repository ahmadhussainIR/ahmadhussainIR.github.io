Know the job type

Compare the offer, not just the title.

The same job title can mean very different daily work. Ask for the real coverage model before judging an opening.

01

Academic radiologist or IR specialist

Look for protected academic time, trainee supervision, research support, promotion criteria, grant infrastructure, procedure mix, call, and whether clinical productivity expectations match academic duties.

02

Private practice or employed hospital role

Clarify partnership track, compensation formula, case volume, weekend and evening call, noncompete rules, governance, vacation, malpractice tail, and how IR clinic or longitudinal care is supported.

03

Locum tenens or teleradiology

Check state licensure, credentialing timeline, malpractice coverage, minimum shift commitment, modality mix, RVU expectations, turnaround time, tech support, and who handles urgent communication.

04

Resident or fellow opening

Confirm appointment year, PGY level, ACGME status, board implications, visa support, funding, procedure requirements, and whether the position is a true vacancy or a future recruitment placeholder.

Before signing

Questions worth asking.

These questions help trainees and attendings avoid vague offers and mismatched expectations.

  • 01

    What is the exact clinical mix by modality or procedure, and what percent of the job is IR, DR, clinic, consults, or tumor board?

  • 02

    How many nights, weekends, holidays, and backup shifts are expected, and how are extra shifts compensated?

  • 03

    Is compensation salary, RVU, collections, partnership distribution, productivity bonus, or locums hourly/daily rate?

  • 04

    What benefits, CME, relocation, malpractice tail, licensing support, visa support, and moonlighting rules are included?

  • 05

    For IR jobs, is there clinic time, admitting privilege, longitudinal follow-up, APP support, IR nurses, dedicated rooms, and block time?

  • 06

    For residents and fellows, will the role affect board eligibility, case logs, graduation timing, or future fellowship/job applications?
